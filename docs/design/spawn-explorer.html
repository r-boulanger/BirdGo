Bird list: 55 species hand-picked for mid-coast Maine, to be checked against eBird sightings for the area. Odds come from how common each bird is that month, its rarity, whether it's active at that time of day, its habitat, and how it reacts to the weather (for example, rain brings out robins, storms push sea ducks and gulls toward shore, and hummingbirds hide in the cold).
