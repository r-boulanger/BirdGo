Catch Lab

Three ways catching could work. Pick a bird, try each style a few times, and tell me which feels right for BirdGo. Rarer birds are harder in every style. These are real Brunswick birds with placeholder art.

2. Try a catching style

Quiet Watch

Calm and patient, like real birding. Hold to observe. When the bird glances around, let go or it gets nervous and flies.

Quick Sketch

Fits the journal look best. Drag your finger through the dots in order before time runs out. Your sketch becomes the journal entry.

Focus Lens

The most "gamey". Like focusing binoculars: tap when the needle is in the sharp zone. Three good taps and it's logged.

Tell me: which style you like, or a mix (for example "Watch to approach, then Sketch to log it"), and whether it's too easy or too hard.
