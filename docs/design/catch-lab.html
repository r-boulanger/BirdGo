Seed Toss

Swipe up to throw a linen seed pouch. Land it inside the ink ring around the bird; the smaller the ring when you throw, the better your odds. A small-ring throw is "Tuffington!", a regular one is "Tuff!", and a miss gets an "Oy vey!" or "Shucks!". The pouch wobbles up to three times, then the bird is logged, or slips out.
